## Bandcamp Librarian: Data & Business Intelligence Visualizations

This repository contains the initial business intelligence reports, dimensional models, and geospatial time-series visualizations engineered for the Bandcamp Dance Librarian project.
The analytical assets housed here project the project's aggregate longitudinal dataset (6,713 tracks across 38 global underground techno labels spanning 1993–2021) to analyze regional cross-genre matrix distributions and urban stylistic trajectories.

<br/>

### 📚 Research Context & Validation
These data and visualization layers provided the foundational analytics behind the case studies presented in the peer-reviewed book chapter:

Vitos, B. (2026). "Genre Classification in Electronic Dance Music Culture: From Localised Histories to the Bandcamp Underground." In H. C. Rietveld & T. Young (Eds.), The Cambridge Companion to Electronic Dance Music. Cambridge University Press.

🔗 Publication DOI: https://doi.org/10.1017/9781009215817.012 .

For further details on the **Core Ingestion & Machine Learning Pipeline**, please refer to the [Bandcamp Librarian repository](https://github.com/bvitos/bandcamp_librarian).

<br/>

### 📊 1. Power BI Interactive Dashboard

The primary interactive business intelligence report is built using Power BI to provide high-performance, low-latency relational data exploration. The report implements dimensional modeling to map algorithmically identified stylistic clusters against global distribution metrics, with a specific focus on the spatiotemporal trajectories of techno labels hosted on Bandcamp. However, by integrating other music genre classification models into the core pipeline (e.g., Essentia TensorFlow audio predictors), the visualization can be extended to a wider range of indie music genres too.

🔗 Live Production Dashboard: [available here](http://bclibvisual.vitos.tv/).

💾 Source File Included: `bandcamp-lib-visual.pbix` (open in Power BI Desktop).

<br/>

### 🗺️ 2. Tableau Geospatial Timeline Animation

To capture the geographic and temporal expansion of the data landscape across three decades, a specialized time-series engine was configured in Tableau.

- Engineering Choice: Due to the heavy rendering overhead and known latency constraints associated with public cloud data engines on complex spatial rows, the complete interactive dashboard was optimized and compiled into a one-minute animation to guarantee a smooth, non-blocking operational presentation.

The timeline animation visualizes the cumulative year-over-year track distribution volume arriving from independent labels, alongside localized urban pie-charts mapping inner-city cluster concentrations.

### Further Datasets

The raw structural files generated by the primary Python data pipeline are included for evaluation:

- 📄 `labels.csv`: Complete listing of underlying record labels, including indexed home city and country markers.
- 📄 `tracks.csv`: Complete relational catalog of the 6,713 processed tracks, detailing algorithmically identified clusters, mathematical distance-to-centroid values, and metadata markers.
